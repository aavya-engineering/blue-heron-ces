# Personnel Security Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes personnel security requirements for **{{org.legal_name}}** in accordance with NIST SP 800-171 Rev 2 (Family 3.9) and CMMC {{si.target_cmmc_level}} requirements.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party personnel who access CUI systems within the {{si.boundary_strategy}} boundary.

## 3. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Name | Responsibility |
|------|------|----------------|
| HR Representative | {{personnel.hr_representative.name}} | Conduct screenings and manage personnel actions |
| ISSO | {{personnel.isso.name}} | Define access requirements based on role sensitivity |
| System Owner | {{personnel.system_owner.name}} | Authorize CUI access based on screening results |

## 4. Policy Statements

### 4.1 Personnel Screening (3.9.1)

- All individuals with access to CUI systems shall be screened prior to granting access
- Screening shall be commensurate with the sensitivity of the information accessed
- Rescreening shall occur at the frequency required by applicable regulations

### 4.2 Personnel Actions (3.9.2)

Upon termination or transfer of personnel with CUI access:
- System access shall be revoked within 24 hours of separation
- All organizational credentials, badges, and keys shall be collected
- All CUI materials in the individual's possession shall be returned
- Exit interviews shall address continuing confidentiality obligations
- Knowledge transfer of security-critical information shall be completed before departure

Upon transfer within the organization:
- Access shall be reviewed and adjusted to match new role requirements
- Access no longer needed shall be revoked promptly

## 5. Third-Party Personnel

- Third-party personnel requiring CUI access shall meet the same screening requirements
- Non-disclosure agreements shall be executed before granting CUI access
- Third-party access shall be reviewed annually and revoked when no longer needed
