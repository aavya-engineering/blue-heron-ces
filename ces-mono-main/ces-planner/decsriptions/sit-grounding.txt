C) Per-answer capture support — “Answer Quality Controls”

Every time an answer is entered, show a small “Answer Quality” card (inline under the input) with:

Basis / Provenance control

A required pick: Client-stated / Documented / Demonstrated / Consultant inference / Unknown

Map this to the tagging policy (FACT/INFERENCE/OPEN_QUESTION). 

domain CES Assessor Approach Kn…

Confidence

High / Medium / Low (defaults to Medium)

Low confidence triggers an auto “follow-up required” flag.

Completeness rubric

Simple rubric: “Specific / Measurable / Evidence-linked / Scope-aware”

If missing, highlight what’s missing:

“You named a tool but not coverage”

“You described policy but not operationalization”

“You asserted control exists but no evidence pointer”

Evidence preview (optional attachments)

Allow attaching optional contextual artifacts even in SIT:

policies, screenshots, diagrams, contracts, org charts

Clearly label these as “context artifacts (non-evidence yet)” to avoid confusion.

Downstream impact preview

Show what this answer will influence later (not generate):

“This will change scoping categories”

“This will change evidence expectations”

“This will affect which RR modules are required”
This is how you keep the consultant oriented without mixing SIT with RR execution.

D) “Do we have enough to move on?” guardrails (soft gates at question level)

On every question page, show a small checklist that must be satisfied before “Next” is enabled (unless question optional):

Basis/provenance selected (FACT/INFERENCE/OPEN_QUESTION)

If OPEN_QUESTION: follow-up recorded

If the question is scope-critical: a scope note is captured

This is consistent with your governance emphasis and the schemas’ pervasive tagging policy requirement.
