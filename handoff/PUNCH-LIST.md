Items those sources show as fixed are left out. `KNOWN-ISSUES.md` holds the symptoms you will meet while running; this file holds the work.

**How to read a row**
- **Id**: `PL-nnn` (punch-list item nnn). Ids are stable; a closed row stays, marked closed.
- **Owner**: **contractor** (engineering work you can do), **BHD owner** (BHD does or grants it: builds, re-baselines, access), **founder decision** (BHD's founder must rule before anyone builds it; do not guess the answer).
- **Priority**: **P1** blocks correctness or acceptance; **P2** fix before any live tenant; **P3** nice to have.
- **Evidence**: where the item is described. `ces\...` and `pkg\...` paths are in this monorepo. Names like `LOG-PH13-LV.md` or `SPOTCHECK-DEPTH-10.4.md` are BHD planning logs (`docs/context/finish/` in BHD's strategy repo, not shipped); ask the BHD owner for any you need. A "§" is a section of that log.
- Control ids such as `PS.L2-3.9.2[b]` are CMMC Level 2 practices and their assessment objectives (NIST SP 800-171A); the bracket is the objective letter. Registry entry ids such as `SI.L1-3.14.4.av-signatures` name one Stamper registry entry (`ces\ces-stamper\src\registry\cmmc-l2-commands.registry.json`; `AZ-*` entries are in `infra-cmmc-l2-commands.registry.json`).
